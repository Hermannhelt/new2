About Ken

Ken has worked as a professional photographer for almost two decades, taking on assignments and projects that have brought him to some of the most remote corners of the world. His images, rich with expression, emotion, and body language, tell authentic stories about people and humanity, and have been featured in numerous magazines and advertising campaigns worldwide. Ken has received numerous awards and is a previous Hasselblad Master, one of the most prestigious photography awards.

Passionate about individuals and their unique histories, he explores the fragile balance between people and their environment, between tradition and modernity. Ken’s photography seamlessly weaves cultural and political content, blending documentary and cinematic portraiture. His application of lighting, combining artificial with natural, takes the images into a surreal space, creating dreamlike visions of everyday people.

Who is this workshop for?

This workshop is designed for both amateur and professional photographers, as well as anyone passionate about photography who wishes to enhance their ability to create stunning images. Participants should have a basic understanding of their cameras and equipment, including basic post-production knowledge. The main focus of the workshop will be portraiture and working with light in both studio and on location.

Workshop objective

Ken will help you discover your unique photographic vision by sharing his extensive experience. Whether you're an amateur or professional, he will guide you in defining your style, lighting techniques and enhancing your creative process. Learn how to sculpt your unique visual aesthetic and craft a distinct, captivating style all your own.

This workshop adopts a relaxed, hands-on style, focusing on mastering and understanding light's role in photography. It blends technical skill with a poetic appreciation of lighting, offering extensive practical experience and personalized feedback.

Participants will engage in shooting exercises across various studio and locations, experimenting with different lighting techniques and sources, including natural light, flash, and continuous light. Additionally, group discussions will provide a platform for sharing and giving feedback.

Focus of the workshop

Mastering Light
Experiment with different lighting techniques and sources, and learn how they affect your image and help you find your personal voice.

Mastering Portraiture
Delve into the art of portraiture. Ken will be sharing his personal approach and offering practical tips for connecting with subjects in new environments. We will use each other as subjects and have one shoot with a model.

Crafting Your Vision Through Editing and Post Production
Discover the art of digital processing with Ken as your guide. Learn essential techniques for maximizing the potential of your digital files and refining your unique photographic style using editing software.

So why Sweden?

This workshop takes place in an ideal setting – Ken’s house Strömsborg by the lake, surrounded by the stunning Swedish countryside in Småland, just two hours from Copenhagen Airport. The holiday house accommodates up to 20 people, offering ample space. Additionally, there is a Yoga Shala that doubles as a photo studio, as well as a sauna and beautiful natural surroundings for relaxation between sessions. Hosting the workshop here helps keep costs reasonable by eliminating hotel expenses.
