Information

“Ken Hermann’s images are pathways, connecting us to micro worlds and challenging us to rethink ideas of photographic representation.

Ken doesn’t worry about trends, staying true to his point of view as an image-maker. It’s exciting to see a young photographer with such a strong singular vision and approach.

Passionate about individuals and their unique histories, he explores the fragile balance between people and their environment, between tradition and modernity. Ken’s photography seamlessly weaves cultural and political content, blending documentary and cinematic portraiture. His application of lighting, combining artificial with natural, takes the images into a surreal space, creating dreamlike visions of everyday people.”

2. The works

All prints are archival pigment prints in limited editions. Each print is hand-signed and numbered and comes with a certificate of authenticity.

  • The edition size for each print size is stated on the print's page, for example 50, 7 + 2 AP or 3 + 1 AP. AP stands for artist's proof.
  • Prints are sold unframed unless the print's page says otherwise. Custom framing can be arranged on request at an extra cost.
  • Colours on screen can differ slightly from the printed work, because screens display colour differently.

3. Prices and payment

All prices are in Danish kroner (DKK) and include 25% Danish VAT.

  • Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay.
  • For deliveries outside the EU, import duties and local taxes may apply. These are paid by the buyer.
  • Payment is made by card through Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your card is charged when you complete your order.

6. Right of withdrawal

As a consumer you have 14 days to withdraw from your purchase without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day you receive the print.

  1. Tell us before the 14 days have passed, by email to ken@kenhermann.dk.
  2. Send the print back no later than 14 days after you told us. You pay the cost of returning it. Pack it as it arrived, so it is not damaged on the way.
  3. We refund the full amount, including the original standard shipping cost, within 14 days of receiving your message. We may wait to refund until the print has arrived back. The refund is made to the card you paid with.

You may handle the print as you would in a shop to look at it. If it has been framed, mounted, written on or otherwise handled more than that, we may deduct the loss in value from the refund.

The certificate of authenticity must be returned with the print.

7. Faults and damage

You have a 2-year right to complain about faults under the Danish Sale of Goods Act. The fault must have been there when you received the print.

  • Damaged in transit: check the package when it arrives. If the print is damaged, take photos of the print and the packaging and email them to ken@kenhermann.dk within 7 days.
  • Other faults: email us within a reasonable time after you discover the fault. Within 2 months is always considered in time.
  • We will reprint, replace or refund the print. We cover the shipping costs for a valid complaint.

8. Editions and copyright

Once an edition is sold out, that print is not reprinted in the same size. Artist's proofs are kept by the artist or sold separately.

Ken Hermann keeps the copyright to all images. Buying a print gives you ownership of the physical print, not the right to copy, publish or use the image commercially.

9. Personal data

We use your name, address, email and phone number only to handle your order and delivery. We share them only with the carrier and with Stripe for payment.

We keep order details for 5 years, as Danish bookkeeping law requires. You can ask to see or correct your data at any time.

10. Complaints and disputes

If you are not satisfied, please contact us first at ken@kenhermann.dk. We will try to find a solution.

If we cannot agree, you can complain to the Danish Consumer Complaints Board through Nævnenes Hus, Toldboden 2, 8800 Viborg, naevneneshus.dk. If you live in another EU country, you can also contact the European Consumer Centre Denmark.
